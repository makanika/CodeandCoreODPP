05 - Complaints layer

Dual taxonomy, deliberately separated

A complaint is classified once by the Head of Complaints and then follows a different data and access path.

TYPE A

Officer conduct

Complaint against an RSA, IO, or another named officer.

Record
Confidential Internal Affairs File with IAF reference.
Scope
Corruption, solicitation, extortion, evidence tampering, professional misconduct.
Routing
Isolated disciplinary registry. Internal Affairs / IQA only.
Hard rule
Accused officer has zero read access. Every read is logged.
TYPE B

Process / case delay

Complaint about status, custody, delay, or communication.

Record
Case Latency Inquiry Sheet linked to CRB and ODPP identifiers.
Scope
Lost file, perusal over 14 days, decision not communicated.
Routing
Regional Inspectorate and RSA mandated status audit.
Hard rule
Case link and elapsed time drive automatic alerts and escalation.

06 - Django system implications

Build the visibility that paper cannot provide

The UI mirrors the ledgers, but the data model makes custody, delay, and responsibility auditable.

Canonical case identity

One Case object with nullable, uniquely indexed sd_reference, crb_reference, odpp_reference, and court_reference.

Custody events

Append-only FileMovement: from_location, to_location, courier, dispatched_at, and received_at. Unreceived dispatches signal loss.

Perusal decisions

PerusalMinute choices: SANCTIONED, DFI, CLOSED; with attorney, directive, and register. Never overwrite a decision.

Complaint isolation

Separate ConductComplaint and ProcessComplaint models. Conduct reads are restricted and audited; process complaints compute latency from registry intake.

Jurisdiction routing

Region -> RSA station -> RSP station hierarchy. Complaint routing derives the responsible regional officer from the case station.

Assisted lodging

Designated police terminals generate a receipt, hashed PIN, and non-sensitive QR locator. The public can lodge or track a complaint, not browse case details.
