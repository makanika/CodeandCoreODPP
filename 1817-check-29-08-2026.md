# ODPP Progress Check

**Checked:** 18:17, 29 August 2026
**Status:** Scope re-confirmed and built out: the system tracks a complaint's underlying case through the full police-to-judgement journey, not just up to sanction. One real workload-display bug found and fixed along the way. App connectivity audited — most of the system is wired end to end; two apps remain deliberate, unconnected stubs.

## Scope Confirmation

Re-stated instruction: this is a **complaints management system that follows a complaint through the prosecution process** — police station → ODPP review → court → judgement — not a system that manages the prosecution itself (no charge drafting, evidence management, trial scheduling, witness handling). `CaseReference` stays a lightweight reference ledger, as previously agreed; today's change deepens how far that ledger reaches, not what kind of system it is.
